Questions

FAQ

How does delivery work?

Balloons travel by taxi: you meet the car and take the order. Large orders and decor we bring ourselves and install on site.

How fast can you deliver?

Urgent — within 1.5–2 hours depending on the area. A specific time can be agreed in advance.

What are your hours?

Daily 8 AM – 11 PM. Delivery after 10 PM is at the late rate (+300 UAH), after 11 PM — by arrangement.

How long do balloons float?

Hi-Float latex — usually 3–5 days, untreated — about a day. Foil — one to two weeks. A lot depends on temperature and humidity.

Can you print any text?

Yes. We print on our plotter in any font and film colour. Type your text in the builder — we’ll make it exactly like that.

Will you send a photo before delivery?

Yes, if you like — we’ll photograph the order before dispatch. Handy when the balloons are a surprise for someone else.

Can balloons go out in the cold?

Yes, we pack them in special bags. In the cold latex shrinks a little and expands again in the warmth.

How can I pay?

Bank card transfer or cash on pickup — whatever suits you.
